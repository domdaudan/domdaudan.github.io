Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-19

This app is a personal, single-user automation tool. It does not collect, store, or share personal data from any third party.

  • The only TikTok data accessed is the OAuth access/refresh token needed to publish video content to the developer's own connected TikTok account.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any other party or service.
  • Video files and access tokens are stored locally on the developer's own machine, not on any third-party server.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account's connected-apps settings, which immediately invalidates the stored token.

Questions about this app can be raised via its GitHub repository.
